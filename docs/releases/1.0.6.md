**New in 1.0.6**

A small update: a show's seasons count what is really on Plex, and phone-app alerts are sent only by the install they belong to.

**Before you update**

- **Phone-app alerts:** `APP_PUSH=expo` no longer works on its own. The app's alerts go through the Plexbie project's own Expo account, so only the project's own install, with that account's `EXPO_ACCESS_TOKEN`, can send them. A Plexbie with `APP_PUSH=expo` and no token now sends nothing to Expo and says so in the log at start-up. Leave `APP_PUSH` and `EXPO_ACCESS_TOKEN` empty: your members get the website's alerts, which your Plexbie sends itself and which work on any phone, including from Safari on a Home Screen. The app points them there.

**Fixed**

- **Season counts:** a show's page now counts each season's episodes from Plex. A season with episodes on Plex that Sonarr never imported (a library older than Sonarr, for example) showed "0 of 70 on Plex" while Seerr called it partly available; it now shows how many Plex has, such as "37 of 70 on Plex". Sonarr's count is used only when Plex can't be reached. Episodes Sonarr has downloaded count once Plex has added them. ([#7](https://github.com/NovaOra/plexbie/issues/7))

**Update**
- Unraid: press Update on the container (it installs `:latest`).
- Docker: pull `ghcr.io/novaora/plexbie:latest`, or pin `ghcr.io/novaora/plexbie:1.0.6`.
- The phone app updates separately: see its [releases](https://github.com/NovaOra/plexbie-app/releases).
