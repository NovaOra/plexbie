**New in 1.0.7**

Tickets for anything, not only for requests: a film or show that's on Plex, or something that isn't about one title. And the server owner no longer takes a top-three place.

**New**

- **A ticket on any film or show on Plex:** its page on the website and in the app has *Something wrong? Open a ticket*, for titles a member didn't request: it won't play, the picture or the sound is wrong, it's the wrong film or episode, episodes are missing, or something else. Plexbie tells the admins what it can see: the file Radarr or Sonarr has (quality, HDR or Dolby Vision, size) and how that member last played it, from Tautulli (on what, played directly or converted, and in what picture). On Manage → Tickets it links to the title's page and to Plex, and the search buttons work on it too.
- **Need help? on Home:** a general ticket, for anything that isn't about one title (Plex itself, an account, something you can't find), and every ticket of yours, open or solved in the last 30 days, to read and add to. An alert about one of them opens it there.
- **`/ticket` in Discord:** pick a film or show on Plex as you type, or **General**, then what's wrong. It's for Plex members (the member role), as on the website, and admins answer it the usual way.
- **Limits:** each member has one open ticket per title (the one on their request for it counts) and up to three general ones, and tickets and answers share five a day. A member can add to an open ticket at any time, not only when an admin asks something.

**Changed**

- **Inactivity:** the server owner no longer takes a top-three place. The three biggest watchers other than the owner are never removed for inactivity, and Manage → People no longer marks the owner "Top three".

**Fixed**

- An admin's alert about a new ticket now opens that ticket. It opened Manage → Requests.

**Update**
- Unraid: press Update on the container (it installs `:latest`).
- Docker: pull `ghcr.io/novaora/plexbie:latest`, or pin `ghcr.io/novaora/plexbie:1.0.7`.
- The phone app updates separately: its 1.0.7 has the new ticket screens (see its [releases](https://github.com/NovaOra/plexbie-app/releases)). Older apps keep working, without them.
