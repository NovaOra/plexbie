**New in 1.0.9**

A fix for cleanup and request expiry: watching a show counts again.

**Fixed**

- **Watching a show counts again:** Plex's watch history stopped saying which show an episode belongs to, so every show looked unwatched to Plexbie. Request expiry turned Sonarr monitoring off for shows people were still watching, so new episodes stopped downloading, and cleanup could warn about a show that only other members had been watching, then remove it. Plexbie now works out the show for each play itself: from the episode, from the show's link when Plex includes it, or from the show's title in that library. When two shows in a library share a title, both count as watched.
- **Films without their link in the history** are found by their title the same way.

**After updating**

- At the next daily check, shows that request expiry turned off are turned back on if anyone has watched them in the last 90 days.
- Titles that were only due for removal because their viewing wasn't counted are no longer due, and their countdown on the website moves back.

**Update**
- Unraid: press Update on the container (it installs `:latest`).
- Docker: pull `ghcr.io/novaora/plexbie:latest`, or pin `ghcr.io/novaora/plexbie:1.0.9`.
- The phone app doesn't change in this release. Its 1.0.8 keeps working.
