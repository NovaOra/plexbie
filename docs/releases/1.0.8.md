**New in 1.0.8**

A new look for the website, a Manage page built as a desk, and alerts that open the one thing they're about.

**New**

- **Sign in on the TV set:** the public page is a little retro TV. Channel 1 signs you in with Discord or Plex, channel 2 shows how to get in, and the same steps sit under the set. Problems signing in show on the screen in plain words.
- **Manage is a desk:**
  - Five lit monitors (Requests, Help, People, Library and Services) show what needs you at a glance.
  - Below them, one queue (Needs you, Waiting and Done) holds every request, ticket, blocked import, stuck download and person that's waiting on an admin.
  - Open an item to deal with it, in a pane beside the queue on wide screens or in a sheet on phones.
  - Everything you need less often is under **Everything else**: invites, joins, cleanup, messages, sign-ins, Discord, all requests, people, health and tickets.
- **Posters everywhere:** tickets, blocked imports and titles leaving soon show their poster, and so do a member's own tickets.
- **Take it and let it go** say which one you meant, so pressing twice, or two admins at once, can't undo a take.
- **Alerts open the item:** an admin's alert for a request or a blocked import opens it on Manage's desk (or its Done row once someone has dealt with it).
- **Tickets Plexbie opens itself** read as Plexbie's: "Plexbie, for Jordan". This covers an import it won't take, a search that found nothing, a wrong film, and a download that failed. The member still owns the ticket. Older tickets are shown the same way.

**Changed**

- **One look across the site:**
  - Keys you can press have a lip, and quieter buttons are hollow pills.
  - Each view has one pink key, for the main thing to do.
  - Spacing and type follow one scale.
  - Every control shows focus clearly.
- **Hold to confirm:** press once and the key says "Press again to decline" (or whatever it does), then press again. Holding still works.
- **Pages hold still while they load:** loading parts keep their shape, so nothing jumps when it arrives.
- **Home:**
  - The channel gives up after 8 seconds if Plex is slow, and says so with a Try again.
  - Search is ready at once.
  - An empty channel stays small.
- **Alerts page:** one pink key for the device you're on. That's Download on Android, Add to SideStore on an iPhone, and Turn on alerts on a computer.
- **Manage** asks search engines not to index it.

**Fixed**

- Keyboard focus no longer falls to the top of the page when a key is busy or goes away, in Manage, Cleanup and the sign-in set.
- Long names and labels are no longer cut off on narrow phones.

**Update**
- Unraid: press Update on the container (it installs `:latest`).
- Docker: pull `ghcr.io/novaora/plexbie:latest`, or pin `ghcr.io/novaora/plexbie:1.0.8`.
- The phone app updates separately. Its 1.0.8 has the same new look, the Manage desk and the TV sign-in (see its [releases](https://github.com/NovaOra/plexbie-app/releases)). Older apps keep working.
